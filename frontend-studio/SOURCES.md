# Sources and attribution

Frontend Studio organizes its own executable workflows and original example code. This release's English guidance is adapted from the project's Chinese working edition; research summaries 13–15 replace the longer working drafts, rather than bundling upstream instructions or checklist text. A source listed here is methodological context, not a claim of dependency, endorsement, or controlled benchmark superiority.

Check the exact upstream file/version and resource rights when importing; this source list does not grant rights over third-party material. Do not copy branding, customer facts, private source, or another skill's instruction text.
