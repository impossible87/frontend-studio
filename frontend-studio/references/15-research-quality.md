# Quality research: provenance summary

This public edition retains the purpose of the Chinese research notes without reproducing long upstream checklist collections. Current 00/07/08/12 are authoritative; consult upstream documents when needed.

Research includes Web Interface Guidelines, Vercel agent skills, web-quality-skills, UI Skills, shadcn/ui, and Playwright. See SOURCES.md. Useful principles are actionable issue locations, semantic interaction, version-aware implementation, evidence-based performance, and preservation of business behavior during redesign.

Static checks detect limited risks. Rendering, keyboard interaction, failure cases, and appropriate measurements must substantiate claims. Label unrun tests. Public demos are original samples, not proof of general generation quality or a benchmark against other tools.
