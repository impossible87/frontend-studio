# Creative transformation: the mechanism belongs to the content

Read the observation record from 17; do not repeat search. Important sections carry a central claim, capability, or conversion task. Ordinary navigation/form feedback does not require two concepts per control.

For each important section write two short concepts, each 2–3 sentences: how information is organized, what state changes, and how the user acts. Compare theme fit, reading/operation, implementation/dependency/layout cost, and page-wide consistency. Avoid pseudo-precise scores. Choose automatically inside the user's direction and authorized scope; ask only for new material business tradeoffs. A running prototype may justify revising the choice.

Preserve a useful reference relationship while adapting content, information structure, layers, type roles, trigger, and timing. Recoloring alone is insufficient, but changing every dimension just to be different is also unnecessary. Keep one visual language.

| Content task | Concept A | Concept B | Decision context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product: understand input→delivery | Scroll synchronizes explanation/demo states | Direct capability controls switch the demo | Brand narrative can suit A; frequent tools often suit B; provide direct controls either way |
| Studio: browse then inspect work | In-place expansion reflows nearby work | Shared-element transition into detail | Selected small collections can suit A; archives can suit B |
| Research tool: compare evidence views | Tabs switch a panel with local height continuity | Side-by-side reading with linked highlights | Separate views can suit A; simultaneous factual comparison can suit B |

These are mechanism entrances, not a fixed effect menu. Different themes need different information relationships rather than the same fade-up with changed copy. Keep a primary focal point and consistent alignment.

Build one representative prototype with real copy lengths. Prefer existing capabilities or CSS/SVG/native animation; use Motion/GSAP only as needed for complex work. Keep the framework. Do not introduce WebGL/3D/particles by default.

Record purpose→two concepts→comparison→choice→reference/original adaptations→prototype result→propagation scope in `design-plans/references.md`. Verify initial/middle/end, reverse/rapid input, keyboard, reduced motion, initialization failure, resize, and cleanup. Interrupted layouts converge on the latest state; text stays readable, focus remains logical, and main actions never wait for a sequence. Capture actual recording or controlled browser frames. State performance observations and unmeasured areas honestly.
