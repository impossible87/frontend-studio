# Aesthetic research: provenance summary

This public edition replaces the long Chinese working research draft with an original English summary. It is not a bundled copy of another skill's instruction text. Current SKILL.md and 00–12/16–18 are authoritative.

Research informed four useful decisions: brief before decoration, concrete visual direction, coherent typography/layout, and critique based on observable problems. Sources include Anthropic frontend-design, Taste Skill, and Impeccable; see SOURCES.md for links.

Use axes such as density, hierarchy, alignment, material treatment, imagery, contrast, and motion purpose to describe a direction. Avoid fixed hex/font recipes and mechanical bans on familiar styles. A reference list is not evidence of a controlled comparison or a ranking against other skills. Choose based on the actual project and inspect the result.
